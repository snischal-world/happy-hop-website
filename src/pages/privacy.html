Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Happy Hop Holidays collects when you enquire or travel with us, why we collect it, who sees it and what choices you have.

In short
  • We collect only what we need to plan and run your trip.
  • We share it with the hotels, drivers, embassies and partners who deliver it.
  • We never sell your information.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete it at any time.

1Who we are

Happy Hop Holidays (“we”, “us”) is a destination management company arranging travel in Bali, Vietnam, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand. We are the controller of the personal information described here.

Happy Hop Holidays is operated by Happy Hop Holidays (ABN 57 594 154 845). You can reach us at happyhopholidays@gmail.com or through the enquiry form on our website.

2What we collect

We collect only what we need to plan and run your trip or event.

  • Enquiry details: your name, company, email, phone number, who you are (traveller, corporate or travel company), what you are planning, destinations, travel month, number of travellers and your message.
  • Booking details: names of travellers as on their passports, dates of birth, passport details and photos where a visa or hotel needs them, flight details, room and dietary needs, and accessibility or health information that you choose to tell us so we can look after you properly.
  • Business partners: company and contact details of travel companies and corporate clients, and the rates and terms agreed with them.
  • Website data: the website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • Social media: if you message or tag us on Instagram or Facebook, those platforms handle that data under their own policies.

3How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare quotes and itineraries.
  • To book hotels, transfers, local transport, restaurants and experiences.
  • To file visa applications when you ask us to.
  • To take payments, issue invoices and keep records the law requires.
  • To support you during your trip, including in an emergency.
  • To improve our service and, with your consent, tell you about new trips. You can opt out at any time.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performing a contract with you, our legitimate interest in running the business, meeting legal duties, and your consent where we ask for it.

4Who we share it with

We share information only as far as needed to deliver your trip:

  • hotels, drivers, guides, restaurants, venues and activity operators in your destination;
  • embassies, consulates and immigration authorities for visa filing;
  • payment providers, accounting and IT service providers who work for us under confidentiality;
  • airlines and ticketing partners, where we book on your behalf;
  • authorities, courts or advisers where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information. Because your trip takes place abroad, these recipients may be in a different country from you. We share the minimum needed and choose partners who protect it.

5International transfers

We work across several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we use them, such as contractual protections with our partners.

6How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months if no booking follows. Booking and payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Passport and visa documents are deleted from our systems once the visa is issued or the trip is complete, unless the law or a dispute requires us to keep them. We then delete or anonymise the information.

7How we protect it

We limit access to people who need it, use access controls on our systems and ask partners to handle your information with care. No system is completely secure, so please do not send card numbers or passwords by email or social media. If a breach is likely to affect you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask what information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct it or ask us to delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example to marketing;
  • ask for your information in a portable format;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, contact us at happyhopholidays@gmail.com. We may need to confirm who you are first, and we will reply within the time the law allows.

9Children

Our website is for adults. We collect information about children only when a parent or guardian books a family trip, and only what we need for the booking.

10Links to other sites

Our site links to Instagram and Facebook. We are not responsible for how those sites handle your information, so please read their policies.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If the change is significant, we will say so on our website.
