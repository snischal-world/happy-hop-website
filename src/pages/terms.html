3Enquiries and quotes

An enquiry is not a booking. A quote shows the services and price we propose, and it stays open for the period it states. Prices depend on dates, availability and exchange rates, so they can change until you confirm and pay. A booking is confirmed only when we send you written confirmation and have received the payment due.

4Prices and estimates

Prices on our website are estimates per person in Australian dollars (AUD). Each is based on one specific package: a particular set of 4-star hotels, private airport transfers, and activities with tickets and transport, in the destination shown. The price you pay is the price in your written quote, and it can be higher or lower than the website estimate.

Prices change because of:

  • the hotels, room types and hotel level you choose, including 3-star, 5-star and resort stays. The package is built around specific hotels, so if you ask to change them, activities or transport, the price is likely to change;
  • your travel dates, including public holidays, festivals, school holidays and other special days, which can carry surcharges;
  • peak-season surcharges;
  • differences between hotels, vendors and partner suppliers, whose rates and terms vary;
  • the number and ages of travellers;
  • higher fuel, transport and supplier costs, including increases caused by conflicts, global events or rising fuel prices;
  • exchange rates, taxes and fees.

Website prices are a guide and not an offer. Nothing is confirmed until we send you written confirmation. If a supplier raises its price or adds a surcharge for reasons outside our control before you travel, we will tell you as soon as we know and explain why before asking you to pay anything extra.

7Changes and cancellations

Tell us as early as you can if you need to change or cancel. Because we pay hotels and suppliers on your behalf, their cancellation and change rules apply to your booking, and we pass the applicable charges to you. Your quote or confirmation will state the charges that apply. We will help you get back whatever a supplier allows to be refunded.

If we need to change or cancel part of your trip, for example because a supplier is unavailable, we will offer a comparable alternative or a refund for the unused part.

When we file a visa for you, we prepare and submit the application using the documents you give us. We do not decide whether a visa is granted, how long it takes, or the fee the authority charges, and we cannot guarantee approval. You must give accurate and complete information and valid documents. Service fees we charge for filing are not refundable if the authority refuses the application, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

9Your responsibilities

  • Hold a passport valid for the period each destination requires, and the right visas and entry documents.
  • Check health, vaccination and safety advice for your destinations.
  • Take out travel insurance covering cancellation, medical care and belongings. We strongly recommend it.
  • Tell us about any medical, dietary or accessibility needs before you travel.
  • Respect local laws, customs, and the staff and property of our suppliers. We may end services without a refund if someone's behaviour puts others at risk.
