Referral terms

The short version, in plain words.

Who can refer

Anyone who has travelled with us and paid in full. Your code is personal to you.

Who can be referred

New customers making their first booking with us. You cannot refer yourself or people travelling on your own booking.

Giving us the code

The code must be given before the booking is confirmed, in the form, by message or by phone. We cannot add it afterwards.

When credit lands

Your Hop Credit is added when your friend's trip is paid in full. If they cancel or are refunded, no credit is issued.

Using Hop Credit

Valid for 12 months, used on one future booking, dollar for dollar. It has no cash value and is not transferable.

Limits

Up to $1,000 of Hop Credit earned per person in any 12 months. Hop Credit can be used together with our current half-price offer, but cannot be combined with other discount codes or partner vouchers on the same booking.

What is not included

Corporate events and travel-company bookings are not part of this offer. Companies can ask us about our partner program.

Fair play

We may decline or reverse credit for misuse, such as fake bookings or posting your code on coupon sites. We may update these terms; the version on the day of booking applies.

Common Questions

When do I get my Hop Credit?
After your friend's trip is paid in full. Enquiries and deposits do not count yet.
Can I refer more than one friend?
Yes. Your code works for any number of friends, up to the yearly limit in the terms.
My friend enquired by WhatsApp or phone. Does it count?
Yes. They only need to quote your code in their first message or call.
Can I use my own code?
No. Bookings made by you or your household do not earn referral credit.
How do I see my Hop Credit?
We email you when your friend books, and send a note when their trip starts. You can also ask us for an update any time.

Got a friend's code?

Add it when you tell us about your trip and we take care of the rest.
